Everyday Asia Explained · Publishing utility

A careful path from finished video to scheduled release.

Everyday Asia Publisher is a Windows desktop utility operated by the owner of the Everyday Asia Explained YouTube channel. It uploads completed episodes, adds selectable subtitles, and schedules approved releases through the YouTube Data API.

For the channel owner's use. This website explains the application and its data practices. It does not offer public account registration, collect passwords, or connect a visitor's Google account.

Upload privately

Send the completed video and its title, description and translated metadata to the authorized YouTube channel.

Add subtitles

Upload 16 prepared subtitle tracks and check their processing status before scheduling.

Schedule a release

Set the approved publication time and read it back from YouTube. YouTube then handles publication.

Why Google access is needed

The utility uses Google OAuth to confirm the channel's identity and obtain permission to upload videos, add captions, and manage the publication settings of its uploads. It requests the youtube.force-ssl scope because YouTube's caption-upload API requires this permission. Google's consent screen describes broader capabilities, including video deletion and comment or playlist management; the current utility does not implement those features.

The application processes the authorized channel ID, upload and processing status, video metadata, subtitle files and caption track IDs. OAuth credentials remain encrypted on the operator's Windows computer. They are not sent to this website or placed in its public source repository.
