Terms of use

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Purpose

Everyday Asia Publisher is a desktop publishing utility operated by Everyday Asia Explained for its own authorized YouTube channel. This informational website does not provide a public publishing service, user registration or a paid subscription.

Authorized operation

The operator must own or have the necessary rights to the channel, video, audio, imagery, captions and metadata submitted to YouTube. Only reviewed episode packages with an approved publication time should be queued. The utility should verify the authorized channel and upload privately before adding captions and scheduling.

Google and YouTube

Use of the utility is also subject to applicable Google and YouTube terms and policies. Google controls OAuth permissions, quotas and API availability; YouTube controls content processing and publication. The utility cannot guarantee uninterrupted authorization, processing time or delivery. The operator should review failures before retrying uncertain operations.

Local operation

The Windows computer must be running, signed in to the configured Windows user, and connected to the internet while an upload job is being performed. Once YouTube confirms a native publication schedule, YouTube handles that release. Revoking permissions or disabling the worker stops future authorized operations but does not automatically remove uploaded content.

Privacy and contact

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. Questions about the utility can be sent to everydayasiaexplained@gmail.com.
